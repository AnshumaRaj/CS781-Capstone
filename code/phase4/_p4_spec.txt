case from the information contained in the case document. The model is expected to understand relevant 
facts, legal arguments, reasoning, and other information presented in the case and use this information to 
predict the final judgment outcome.

labels: 0 and 1 You must use the Legal Language Model developed in Phase 1 as the starting point for

11. Phase 5 - Legal Statute Identification (LSI) 
11.1 What Do You Need to Do? 
Legal Statute Identification (LSI) is the task of identifying which laws or statutory provisions are relevant 
to a given legal document or case. A statute is a law enacted by a legislature, and legal cases may refer to 
one or more statutes that are relevant to the facts, arguments, or decision in the case. 
In this task, you must identify the corresponding statute IDs from the provided set of statutes. Since a 
legal document can involve multiple statutes, this task is formulated as a multi-label classification 
problem. A single document may therefore have several correct statute IDs. 
You must use the Legal Language Models developed and pretrained by you in Phase 1 as the starting 
point for this phase. You must experiment with all four Phase 1 models/architectures using suitable 
fine-tuning or adaptation strategies and submit your best four experiments/submissions involving 
different Phase 1 models. Fine-tuning only one model with different techniques will not be sufficient.

●​ Use a suitable multi-label classification head or prediction mechanism.

●​ Handle long legal documents appropriately. 
●​ Use only the Phase 1 Legal Language Model and the provided competition data.
